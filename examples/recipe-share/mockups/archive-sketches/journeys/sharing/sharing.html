Weeknight Tomato Pasta

Shared at /r/weeknight-tomato-pasta · anonymous viewer · no sign-in

Ingredients

  • 400g canned tomatoes
  • 300g spaghetti
  • 2 cloves garlic

Steps

  1. Sauté garlic in olive oil until fragrant
  2. Add tomatoes and simmer 15 minutes
  3. Boil spaghetti and toss with sauce
